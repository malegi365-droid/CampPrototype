# Ability Design

## Ranger

### Class Fantasy
Precision, positioning, and ranged burst damage.

### Kit
- **Left Click — Basic Attack**
  - Standard ranged attack.

- **Q — Piercing Shot**
  - Persistent ability.
  - Enhances ranged pressure.

- **E — Explosive Shot**
  - Signature ability.
  - Area burst damage.

- **Shift — Dash Arrow**
  - Mobility ability.
  - Fast repositioning.

- **R — Overcharge**
  - Ultimate.
  - Temporarily empowers Ranger damage/output.

---

## Guardian

### Class Fantasy
Force, control, durability, and unstoppable momentum.

### Kit
- **Left Click — Basic Attack**
  - Axe combo melee attacks.

- **Q — Guard Stance**
  - Persistent ability.
  - Reduces damage and reinforces the tank/control identity.

- **E — Sunder Axe**
  - Signature ability.
  - Heavy axe strike with armor break.

- **Shift — Bulwark Charge**
  - Mobility ability.
  - Guardian charges forward, pushing enemies and controlling positioning.

- **R — Last Stand**
  - Ultimate.
  - Guardian becomes an unstoppable juggernaut for a short duration.
  - Reduced damage taken.
  - Cannot be staggered.
  - Basic attacks become cleaving swings.
  - Hits create shockwaves.
  - Generates threat constantly.

---

## Toxicologist

### Class Fantasy
Battlefield control, poison, area denial, and sustain.

### Kit
- **Left Click — Basic Attack**
  - Toxic projectile attack.

- **Q — Rapid Regen**
  - Persistent ability.
  - Heals over time.
  - Enhanced during Containment Failure.

- **E — Poison Cloud**
  - Signature ability.
  - Creates poison area denial.
  - Larger and stronger during Containment Failure.

- **Shift — Toxic Thrusters**
  - Mobility ability.
  - Dash that leaves a poison trail.
  - Reduced cooldown during Containment Failure.

- **R — Containment Failure**
  - Ultimate.
  - Toxicologist becomes unstable and emits poison.
  - Empowers Rapid Regen, Poison Cloud, and Toxic Thrusters.
